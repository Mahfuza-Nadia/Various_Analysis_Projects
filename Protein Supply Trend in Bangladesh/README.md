PROTEIN SUPPLY AGGREGATION METHOD

Source:
FAOSTAT protein supply data for Bangladesh, 2010–2023.

Purpose:
To aggregate individual food items into six selected protein
food groups.

Six food groups:
1. Fish
2. Meat
3. Milk
4. Eggs
5. Pulses
6. Cereals

Method:
Individual food items were assigned to one of the six food groups.

The protein values were then summed by food group and year.

Aggregate rows were excluded where their component items were
already included, in order to avoid double counting.

Milk:
Milk - Excluding Butter was retained as the main measure.

Eggs:
Eggs was retained as the main measure.

Final calculation:
For each food group and year:

Food group protein supply =
Sum of protein supply values for all included items
belonging to that food group in that year.

The resulting six-group dataset was used to calculate:
1. Absolute change from 2010 to 2023
2. Percentage change from 2010 to 2023
3. Protein contribution/share of each food group
4. Animal-source protein share
5. Plant-source protein share
